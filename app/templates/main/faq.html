Find clear answers regarding society waste collection, tracking, and AI route scheduling.

The Society Secretary registers the housing society, sets preferred daily or custom pickup time slots, and submits pickup requests. The system automatically bundles nearby requests into optimized collection routes for dispatch.

To prevent logistics clutter, waste collection is managed by the Society Secretary on behalf of all residents. The Secretary can broadcast arrival updates and share monthly sustainability certificates with residents.

Vehicle GPS telemetry updates every 3 seconds via OpenStreetMap & Leaflet.js. The system computes dynamic traffic delays and provides live distance & ETA countdowns.

Collection workers log the weight of Wet, Dry, Recyclable, and Hazardous waste categories and upload a timestamped photo verification before marking the pickup completed.
